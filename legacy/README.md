# legacy/

Retired work, kept for the record and read-only.

| directory | what it is |
|---|---|
| `experiments/grasp_metrics/` | the grasp-quality benchmark this repository began as: G1–G5, the matched comparison, the peg task, their figures. Its supported findings are summarised at the end of the top-level README. |
| `experiments/retracted/` | experiments whose results were withdrawn, each with a note saying what was wrong; their generators refuse to run. |
| `results/`, `figures/` | the benchmark's results and figures, and `retracted/` for the withdrawn ones. |
| `attic/` | superseded scripts, two kept as retraction evidence. |

Nothing here is imported by the live code. Do not extend it or copy its
patterns; the measurement rules that replaced them are in `CLAUDE.md`.
